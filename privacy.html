Privacy policy

personal secretary · Last updated 23 November 2026

personal secretary is a personal tool used by one person, the owner of the Google account that signs in to it. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads the owner's Gmail messages and labels, and reads and edits the owner's Google Calendar.

How the data is used

  • Only to help the owner: sorting and labelling their mail, keeping notes of deadlines and tasks, and adding reminders to their calendar.
  • Processing happens on the owner's own computer. To read and summarise content, the tool sends it to AI model services chosen by the owner, only for that purpose.
  • Notes the tool keeps are stored on the owner's own computer.

What it does not do

  • It does not sell or share the data with anyone.
  • It does not use the data for advertising.
  • It does not send email on the owner's behalf or permanently delete anything.

personal secretary's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can withdraw access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
